The Wii rollout is now in full effect. I have to get like 50 something points in order to get a special event badge. 1 for a beat, 2 for a mastery, but some games are doubled. I won't show all the games I played, because some of them I only beat. In fact.... Most of them! I beat 21 games for the Wii over the course of a few months, and only mastered 9 of them. Those beats are for more complicated games like Smash Bros, Animal Crossing, Monster Hunter, and Xeno Blade, though I got REALLY close to mastering Xenoblade I just got tired of grinding. And all the games I actually fully mastered are games like uh... this. So don't expect very much Mario or Zelda in this list. This is mostly shovelware.


![Game](https://imgur.com/eDAfTlr.png){: .center-block :}

Every console has to have its game show simulations, right? Good shit. This one is specifically British, which fucked me over a lot. I remember one question about the breeds of Welsh sheep and I was like "this is the most UK thing ever".

Other than that, it's Who Wants To Be A Millionaire. Regis Not Philbin up there asks you a question, you get 4 choices, 3 not fully consistent lifelines (though they never failed me in this game), and like 20 questions until you become a fake millionaire. I just like trivia, honestly.

![Game](https://imgur.com/43EoKuS.png){: .center-block :}

Honestly the best part of this game was the mastery icon. RA devs have a tendency to make game show icons this kinda thing, where they take the host and stretch them zoomed in so it's a block of face. It's silly.
