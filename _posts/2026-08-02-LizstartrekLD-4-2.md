**Dos Cerritos** - LD
8/10

<img src="https://imgur.com/MQaZih0.png" alt="trek">

We start with watching Tendi be badass. She's VERY good at being a pirate! Oh except for one thing: She doesn't like killing people! She's fine with robbing rich assholes, who cares, maybe even roughing them up a little. But killing them is a no go.

Back on the Cerritos, everyone is feeling a little stressed out by their current problems. Oh except Rutherford, he's distracting himself by putting in more and more work. G-good for him. He's doing... VERY well.

<img src="https://imgur.com/0G0BDC5.png" alt="trek">

They go to check out an anomaly and get sucked through to a parallel reality and find another Cerritos with a bunch of parallel versions of themselves. T'Lyn is the same except one says remarkable and one says fascinating. Boimler has a beard. Stuff like that. Oh and the captains dead. whoops. Beckett is now the captain. Things are... slowly unraveling, as it's clear that Captain Beckett is Kinda Evil.

Meanwhile, T'Lyn gets a chance to pay off her debt and return home by salvaging some old ancient warpship. 

<img src="https://imgur.com/LG8uxWg.png" alt="trek">

lol blue Orions from TAS.

Things are collapsing quite quickly in both storylines. The alternate versions are fighting each other, and the pirates turn on Tendi cause she doesn't let them stab them. Captain Beckett also tries to swap places because she's like "wow I used to have fun okay gonna steal your life".

Tendi and the pirates come around with each other cause they have hobbies they want to persue to, like her. Like dancing and baking. That's cute. 

Alternate Mariner is stopped, and Alternate Boimler takes over as acting captain, doing the Riker Chair Thing. That's so good. I think you unlock that move once you get a beard.

<img src="https://imgur.com/FPDl5QK.png" alt="trek">

Tendi earns her freedom but sticks around cause the Orions and the Blue Orions are at war now because of "un-pirate-like methods".

Good start!

Also it turns out her sister was trying to protect TENDI about it cause if she knew it'd mean she'd need to stay for another 14 years or something. I thought she WANTED her to stay? She fucked her over in the last season? Whatever.

Tendi is sent back to Starfleet, and finds out they were just tasked with hauling away "treasure trash", jewels and stuff they were throwing away. **Starbase 80?!** - LD
8.5/10

<img src="https://imgur.com/J8uYxPu.png" alt="trek">

Nice interrobang!

Beckett is feeling weirdly happy and optimistic. But then... the ship breaks down and needs repairs. And the closest starbase is... STARBASE 80?!

Mariner is NOT happy about this. She's sure SB80 is cursed.

Their transporters are not good for organic use soooo we have an Enterprise gel wipe down. The people here also are wearing Enterprise outfits, which is interesting. It's VERY old fashioned here. The story splits. Ransom and hte captain need to get to the head engineer, and are struggling to get there.

Meanwhile, the Lower Deckers are exploring around as some kind of zombie virus takes hold. Mariner is SURE it's cause of the curse, buuuut it turns out to be from the Cerritos!

<img src="https://imgur.com/ENe9xoE.png" alt="trek">

They FINALLY find the engineer at the arcade, he's been sneakily trying to get them to fix problems in the station. He's also voiced by Gideon's dad from Gravity Falls and its very distracting.

This makes Starbase 80 something that doesn't bother me. It kinda lowkey annoyed me before this episode, because it was an evil shitty place that no one wanted to go to, it was basically Bad Person Jail for people. I was like "why would Starfleet HAVE something like this?"

This explains what Starbase 80's deal actually is. It's not cursed, it's not intentionally shitty. It's a place where a bunch of weirdos go and people who need second chances. They're kinda lost in the lurch cause of a diplomatic mess up, giving half the starbase to the control of a "knife gang" who are actually perfectly pleasant people, but it makes things weird. Like there's still the problem with Starfleet ignoring and forgetting them cause they're at the bottom of the priority list, Starfleet incompetence, but this is still SO much better.

They solve the problem and help fix all the problems they can at SB80, which is sweet. I really liked this one.

**Fissure Quest** - LD
7/10

<img src="https://imgur.com/pA2eKBY.png" alt="trek">

Here it is. Season finale. Series finale. Can it cleanly end it all?

A rift opens and hits some Klingons. They turn into Discovery Klingons, that's funny. And horrifying! One asshole Klingon Captain, Relga, who's brothers Ma'ah killed, intends to use this to further her career. They find Ma'ah and try to kill him, but his brother forces them to sneak out.

Meanwhile, Boimler gets the news and tries to get help from the upper command. The captain agrees and tries, and they're like "Yes, this is real. Here, it's up to you, good luck." Also everyone hates Boimler for his clone sending the problem HERE.

Also for some reason Rutherford is being a grumpy pants.

<img src="https://imgur.com/k2kU00s.png" alt="trek">

Ma'ah and his brother show up, requesting asylum. Only Ma'ah's brother actually, he's annoyed by all of this. Relga tries to force them to give it over by pretending to be the admiral, it seems, aaaand then just attacks them anyway. She, like her brothers, is not a very good Klingon. Why is anyone following this person?

They run, cause y'know, theys till need to solve a universal collapse, while Ma'ah's brother disappears. They get close to the field, which will turn people into weird versions of themselves... 

<img src="https://imgur.com/g49UAjE.png" alt="trek">

The ships keep turning into other versions of their ship. One of the Klingon ships is now an old sailing barge and they all die :) Yay. Spacing quota met!

<img src="https://imgur.com/F4Tpfm2.png" alt="trek">

Ma'ah gets angry and thinks where his brother is hiding is hidden in Boimler's padd. Boimler breaks the Padd. It was working for him sure, and he was cool and hot, buuuut it doesn't matter if he doesn't have his friends' backs. Cute.

They manage to defeat Relga by having Olly use her thunder blasts to break their shields. They change and explode, GG.

One final ship change for the Ceritos, aaand it becomes a Galaxy class, sweet. Only for a bit though, it goes back to a shitty Cali-class. Rutherford rips out his implant for some reason and supes up the ship. There's something about how he thinks he Cali class is the best, I dunno.

Okay it's moving so fucking fast and every sub plot is concluding that it's really hard to explain. Whatever, everuything happens. Rutherford falls back in love with the ship, Tendi and T'lyn work together as Science Besties to stop quantum collapse.

<img src="https://imgur.com/eiMkfwa.png" alt="trek">

The rift isn't closed, but is stablized. Now Stafleet can go explore the multiverse as a new frontier. To help the mission, they move Starbase 80 to act as the stop gap between the new rift and Starfleet, cause its old systems aren't effected by the rift's effects.

Captain Freeman is assigned as the new person in charge of this. She's leaving the Cerritos. 

<img src="https://imgur.com/Qyd9fGv.png" alt="trek">

Rutherford is fully human! It's also a tease towards Tendi x Rutherford, which is cute. 

The episode ends with a big cute thing where Mariner talks about how special the crew is and how they're explorers, and not perfect, but a family, etc, etc.

Captain Ransom.... oh no, I'm worried about that. But the adventure continues, etc etc. Boimler and Mariner are also promoted as provisional first officers. They are VERY anxious about this, but everyone seems proud of them. 

I felt like they set up too many things and then knocked em all down well, but it was still paced a little off. I feel it was a little overloaded. And sad it's the ending. But still, mostly good, mostly cute.
