**The Star Gazer** - PIC
5.5/10

<img src="https://imgur.com/g6HG9u6.png" alt="trek">

We get an in media res thing of Picard blowing up his own ship in front of the Borg Queen. Ohhhhh boyyy more BORG QUEEN STUFF, MY FAVORITE CHARACTER IN THIS SERIES! OHHH BOYYYYYYYYYY.

Anyways jump back 48 hours, Picard is hanging on his Chataeu with that one hot Romulan who wants to climb in his pants. Hey, remember how cool she is? How cool the idea of a reformed ex Tal Shiar being on our side is? And how they just put her on a bridge last time? I can't wait for them to fix that problem this time. Picard ALMOST kisses her but stops for some reason. God dammit, Jean Luc.

Anyways we get some Picard childhood flashbacks of him as a... Dickensian whelp? And his mother who is like, tortured and killed in front of him or some shit. I dunno, it's really hard to follow and it keeps jumping to Sir Patrick Stewart sitting there and staring forward, cause like, the dude was 82 when this was shot, we couldn't let him do very much.

<img src="https://imgur.com/ILAMYHv.png" alt="trek">

We then jump to Picard declaring time is the true final frontier in front of some graduating class or something? Look, this makes no sense and is jumping all over the place. The pacing is very bad. Picard for some reason calls out Elnor, the first Romulan in starfleet. Imagine you're graduating and the dude giving the speech is like "shoutouts to my stepson in the audience!" I'd be annoyed.

<img src="https://imgur.com/rNFERei.png" alt="trek">

Seven is flying around in a ship by herself and comes across the Burning Legion, I guess. Some kind of horrifying subspace tear. I'm sure that's not important

Dr. Jurati (I could NEVER remember her name I swear I am trying) is with the android girl and they're having some kind of fancy dinner. Jurati is... kinda unhappy to be here, it feels. The android is staying behind, not gonna be in the show anymore, bye bye. Okay, baiii. Jurati beams up to her ship, which is captain'd by... Rios! He's back in Starfleet, and is a captain now. I guess he's over all the trauma now, good for him. He still looks hot as hell.

Okay so uh, Remember Raffi? And how she held hands with Seven in that one scene? They're dating now. But Raffi is kind of over it already, cause turns out Seven, having the unresistable urge to run off to the asscrack of space and beat up bad guys, is not a very good partner. Who could have known?? At least we KIND of get lesbianism

WE NEED OUR QUOTA OF GUEST STARS. Picard goes to Guinan for advice on his life and romance. JUST KISS HER, JEAN LUC, SHE'S VERY DOWN BAD. He also talks to her about his borg trauma. Okay, first, there's no way he's never talked to her about borg trauma, she was more his therapist than Troi was. Second, can we talk about OTHER things that happened to Picard? The only thing we ever hear is Locutus stuff. Can't we have like... a season about Inner Light?

Anyways turns out the hole in space time has a message coming out of it. "Help us, Picard" and the entirety of article 15, the article about joining the Federation. So they send Picard to these guys to discuss joining the Federation. He teleports out without even saying goodbye to his Romulan Wife and it lingers on her sadly staring at where he just teleported out. 

We get him going on Rios' ship and saying hello into the wormhole. Okay, so, if we DIDN'T have the in media res, we'd be in suspense about this, but we already know it's the Borg, and we already know it's gonna go south. So when Picard is like "let's talk to them :)" and Seven is like "It's the fucking BORG? NO? BLOW THEM UP?" we already know Seven is right, so uh, why are we focusing on this

<img src="https://imgur.com/FujfaiQ.png" alt="trek">

The Borg Queen is like, Doc Oc for some reason, and comes in and starts sucking energy reserves out of their ship. They try to shoot her and it doesn't work at all. She gets command codes for all Starfleet ships for some reason, because Starfleet has fully Borg Upgraded all their ships. Picard blows the ship up to stop it, and the queen goes "look up" just as it blows up and everyone dies. 

Picard wakes back up. Was that line about time being the last frontier really just a setup for a time loop or something? His home is... a little different.

<img src="https://imgur.com/CtGRH0H.png" alt="trek">

HEY LOOK WHO IT IS! John Delancie shows up and is de-aged through tech stuff, and since he's just standing still it actually looks good, then he snaps his fingers to make himself over to "make it fair" or whatever, which is a very Q thing to do. The trial never ends. He welcomes Picard to the "end of the road not taken".

Over all, it was kinda hard to follow, but there's some interesting setups.


**Penance** - PIC
6/10

<img src="https://imgur.com/OrnCVPA.png" alt="trek">

Q is... not well. He's sick. But he does his bullshit he always does, mocking Picard, who is not having it. But then Q SLAPS him. God damn.

Q shows Picard around this alternate universe, "of his own making". He shows Picard his trophy room, which has Sarek, Martok, and Gul Dukat's skulls on display. This is NOT the mirror universe. This is... like, ANOTHER mirror universe? Another evil fascist Federation alternate timeline? Weird. In this one, it's The Confederation.

I find it strange how in this timeline, Earth has basically been alone by itself and has managed to fight against every single person in the Federation AND outside of it, like Romulans, Klingons, and the fucking Borg, and STILL managed to win. We struggled against the Borg as a federation, how did we do this??? I think this is, again, as usual, just pretending fascism is an actual effective government system, which it is not.

<img src="https://imgur.com/2AzB9tq.png" alt="trek">

Even wakes up as herself. She's the president of the Confederacy. She immediately sets off messaging the rest of the crew here to find out what the fuck is going on, liek Rios, who  is under attack and is like "what the fuck is going on". Elnor is ALSO here, waking up as he's being fired at by the Confederation. He immediately runs into Raffi, lucky him. I do find it interesting how in this world, which seems to be born of a single time travel change, every person we know is still born.

Seven visits Dr. Jurati with her husband who is a good little fascist and is like "why are we not celebrating eradication day by killing aliens >:(" and she's like "shut up honey" and he's a good pussy whipped boy and goes "yes dear" and then they go and find that Jurati has the Borg Queen in a jar and she's like "WHAT THE FUCK IS GOING ON", so okay cool she's in on it too. 

<img src="https://imgur.com/gGYUWRO.png" alt="trek">

Everyone meets up. They find out, through the Borg Queen's Calculation Magic, that this is their universe, not an alternate one, just one that Q changed with time travel back in the past, in 2024. Man, what a year that was, huh? Wait shit. Is this the future that comes from Trump's America?? Anyways they need her to calculate how to go back into the past, but not before the Borg Queen is carted off for ERADICATION DAY CELEBRATIONS, yaaaay

They have to pretend to be doing the execution of the Borg Queen while actually trying to sneak her onto Rios' ship. While they're doing their thing, Rios and Jurati have a falling out and break up. Is this the best time for that?

Okay so I hate the Borg Queen, but she's the best she's ever been here, because she's silent and has no agency.

They are almost out but then nope, Seven's shitty fascist husbando shows up ready to kill them, shoots Elnor, and then cliff hanger.



**Assimilation** - PIC
7/10

<img src="https://imgur.com/gAva7qS.png" alt="trek">

So, the episode ended with Elnor bleeding out and them at gun point, so it'll be bad, right? Actually it'll be super easy. Barely an inconvenience. They just... steal the fascist's guns and kill them with them. Okay, why were their phasers on Dust when THEY get shot with them, but they shot Elnor with the Bleed Out feature? 

<img src="https://imgur.com/TYmm3Er.png" alt="trek">

The Queen assimilates the ship and easily blows up the people shooting at them. So uh... how did the fascists defeat the Borg again?

They go back in time, crash land in France. The Queen is knocked out, they want to kill her, but Picard refuses cause she's the best connection to figure out whats going on. I mean, she told you to go see The Watcher in LA, we know who that is. Elnor dies. He COULD be brought back if they fix the timeline, but he's dead for now.

Raffi is devestated, because he was like a child to her. Uh... he was? I dunno if they said two words to each other in season 1. So uh, this is a problem with this show. It basically pretends season 1 does not exist and did not happen. This is fine for some parts, but then they also are like "oh these characters have this connection" which was never seen. All of these connections are... good. They are good ideas. It would have been good if they had been built up. But they were not, and they are not. So it's weird. Apparently fans thought Raffi x Seven was cute and the actors agreed so they put it in here. But it was never built up and it feels strange to just hit you at the end of that relationship. Elnor and Raffi son mother stuff is a great idea, but you can't just act like thats always been a thing.

So like, I knew they were gay in this season, but I expected that they'd in fact, not be in scenes together so they could SAY they were gay but not have to actually be gay.
