Every Sunday my Twitch subs and I watch Star Trek in my Discord. Here are my reviews and thoughts on each of the episodes as I see it. If you're curious about a nerd's views on Star Trek episodes, please read on, I go quite in depth with some of these episodes! I've watched some Star Trek before but not all of it!

This is an interesting one. Fully 3D animated, so the third animated Trek show, and Trek does quite well in animation, though it's never been 3D. This one is ALSO... for kids! That's not a bad thing. Some of the best shows of all time are for kids. I don't expect this to be like, Avatar or Gravity Falls levels, but I expect this to be the real last bright spot in the Trek ouvre.


**Lost and Found** - PRO
7.5/10

<img src="https://imgur.com/T8Ejxm0.png" alt="trek">

We start by seeing Dal R'El, our hero, stuck on a prison colony. He can't communicate with anyone else because translators were banned. He's watched over by big scary robots, who ask him if he's seen "fugitive zero". He has. They are gonna torture it out of him, but then fugitive zero does a big splosion and Dal R'El tries to escape in the progress in a big exciting escape scene whiiiich ends in failure, whoopsies.

We then get the big bad robot working with some girl who communicates with a Kazon. Ooh, Delta Quadrant stuff, huh? She's the daughter of someone important here, "The Diviner", though seems to be just being used by him. She has to translate for them, and slave trades some cute cat creature. She's pissed that it's a kid, and tells off the Kazon. This gets her in kind of hot water with the superbot and her dad, though he lets her off the hook. He's also worried about the Federation for some reason, which is interesting since they're so fucking far away.

<img src="https://imgur.com/CsivP2D.png" alt="trek">

Dal is given a chance to escape. The "progeny", Gweyn, will get him off this rock if he can tell her how to find "Fugitive Zero", who is a Medusan. Medusans were in the TOS episode "Is There In Truth No Beauty?", which is a WILD callback, that's not even a very important episode. They're a telepathic species that drives people mad. 

There's also lots of stuff about Dal not knowing who or what he is. Apparently that's a point of angst for him, not knowing his species. I don't see why that matters that much but I guess I have a different opinion on that kinda stuff.

While mining and looking for Fugitive X, Dal and a big rocky friend he makes enemies with crash into a cave and find a hidden Federation ship just... sitting there. As soon as the ship activates, they can actually communicate. The rocky friend is actually a girl, and a kid, her name is Rak-tak. 

<img src="https://imgur.com/iB0ttvX.png" alt="trek">

Zero shows up and now that they have a BETTER way off this rock, they'll all work together, they just need an engineer to fix it. So they get a Tellarite Engineer named Jankom Pog. God, that cannot be his name. He's poggers, is he?

Rok invites someone she always liked and wanted to communicate with. A cute little slug being. She wanted to know what he's like! He is... not intelligent. That's cute. Pet Slug! His name is Murf :3

Since Dal couldn't find Zero (he doesn't rat them out), he's thrown onto the surface, which is very dangerous and no one survives who's sent there. Gwyn is unhappy he's sent up there but can't do anything about it. So it'll be super hard for him to escape right? Actually it'll be super easy. Barely an inconvenience. Gwyn helps him escape so that he can lead them to Zero.

<img src="https://imgur.com/OmNisSn.png" alt="trek">

What results is a giant battle. They need to escape in the ship while trying to kidnap Gwyn and hold her hostage as a bargaining chip. The ship, "the protostar" as the Diviner calls it, is almost destroyed in the battle but then blasts off.

Dal has to have one last big climactic battle with the big evil robot Drednok who is TOTALLY not General Greivous. I'mma be real, getting BIG Star Wars Clone Wars vibes from this show already. That's not a bad thing.

The day is saved by Murf pressing the phaser button. He's a non sapient weapons specialist :D

They make it out and into space! "Okay so where are we going?" "I have no idea." And then the ship for some reason FINALLY activates his hologram.

<img src="https://imgur.com/SwZQOZR.png" alt="trek">

Hologram Janeway is here. I guess if it's gonna be a Delta Quadrant show she should be here?

For a pilot of Trke, this is up there. Its pacing is a bit weird but hey, it's alright!
