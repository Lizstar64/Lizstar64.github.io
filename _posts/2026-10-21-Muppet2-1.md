My friends and I are watching the Muppet Show on Wednesdays and I guess I'll do a quick review and watchalong. But first, I guess we should talk about the thing about watching the Muppets in the year of our Luigi 2026.

The Muppet Show is a beloved institution, and everyone loves it. So you'd think it'd be super easy to watch today, right? It is NOT. It's been re-released a bunch of times, but due to the nature of what the Muppet Show IS, the re-releases keep shifting and cutting things all over the place. So if you want the FULL experience, it's pretty impossible. But you can do your best.

ANOTHER thing is, what is the order? If you ask Wikipedia, you get a specific answer. If you ask the Muppets wiki, you get another. We're just... gonna do our best to watch them all in whatever order we can, alright? At least it's obvious where the seasons begin and end.

And finally... the whole point of the Muppet Show is about the human guest stars who star alongside the Muppets. These are all the most famous stars of the 70s, so I know like, MAYBE five of them. Obviously, I'm gonna like an episode that stars Vincent Price, who I love, a little more than one that stars Sandy Duncan, who I do not know at all. There might be some jokes and commentary about those guest stars I just flat out do not get. I'm doing my best, but this isn't like, a retrospective. This is a nerd who grew up watching random Muppets content who wants to watch the Muppets Show and the only way she can really connect to media is if she orders and ranks it all or some shit. So yeah, let's do that.

Episode 203: Milton Berle
7/10

<img src="https://imgur.com/o77yfym.png" alt="trek">

Milton Berle was a very classic comic, even at the time. He's fun, I like him trying to do a comedic monologue but getting heckled by The Boys. He doesn't handle em well. If I'm being real, the Statler and Waldorf heckles are already better this season.

Our running plot is that Fozzie is afraid to talk to Berle, so he keeps trying to hide from him. Every other muppet treats him with basically no respect. Eventually Berle manages to coax him out of hiding and they do a big vaudville act.

<img src="https://imgur.com/7qAca0g.png" alt="trek">

First Pigs in Space segment! If we're giving Ms Piggy top billing, we should give her MORE skits!


Episode 204: Rich Little
7/10

<img src="https://imgur.com/9145iOD.png" alt="trek">

Rich Little's thing appears to be impressions, which they lean on a lot. He opens up with impressions of W.C. Fields and Humphrey Bogart. I HAVE seen Casablanca, and uh, I mean, I guess it's an okay impression? I feel I could do one of the same quality, I dunno. But then he does a bunch of impressions of Muppets and it's loads of fun.

One of the best parts of moving past season 1 is Gonzo will become an actual character and not a background side character. I love Gonzo. Our running skit is that Gonzo is trying to do a dancing chickens act and it's not going amazingly. They are, in fact, chickens. They use ACTUAL chickens here, not Henrietta.

The chicken he eventually gets up on stage is named Lolita. Not the name I'd choose for the chicken.

5.5/10

<img src="https://imgur.com/9145iOD.png" alt="trek">

Steve starts to audition as well. Just cause. He's got an EXTREMELY chaotic energy, it's fun to watch. He goes up three times to do random acts like balloon animals or juggling.
