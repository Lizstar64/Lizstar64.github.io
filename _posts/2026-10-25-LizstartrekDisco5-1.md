**Mirrors** - DIS
3/10

<img src="https://imgur.com/DfVbCrj.png" alt="trek">

They reach the next clue on the Davinci Code-ass jounrey they're on, and it's a big-ass wormhole in space. Why have we not explored this already? Wormholes are very important. I watched DS9, that's like the whole fucking point of it. That, and Trills Are Hot And Fascists Suck Ass. Those are the only 3 things you should take away from DS9, yes.

Michael and Book need to go into the wormhole, where they find the mirror Enterprise stuck in interdimensional space. Meanwhile, Rayner is in charge of Discovery for the first time and he's still kind of unlikable. Interesting A and B plot, totally not unfitting side by side.

Book and Michael explore the Enterprise. Michael is like "Oh this is where my brother was. Surely this will not end with her being proven wrong and being sad about it all.

<img src="https://imgur.com/Oa6vBdd.png" alt="trek">

They finally find Moll and L'ak and we learn why they're doing this. They have a "Breen Blood Bounty" on them, we also learn L'ak is Breen, though I spoiled that immediately. Whoops.

Why did they remove the muzzles from the Breen helmets? Didn't want Dog Goo People? That's what this series was missing. If L'ak was a doggy goo girl, I'd give every episode a 10. Your loss, Discovery.

we then start jumping back and forth from the flashback to present. Weird, dunno why? This episode is very Clap Nerds, cause y'know, it expands on Breen Lore. The Breen were a part of late DS9, but we never got a LOT about them or their culture or their people. We literally never heard them speak a language we could understand. We learn that like.... they wear these suits to keep goo, otherwise they solidify, and it's the Breen Way to be goo. Being solid is "inflexible and weak". That's why we wear these EXTREMELY inflexible suits. What's the point in being goo if you're goo in a fucking solid thing anyway?! That's so dumb?!

We also learn that Book's mentor, and Moll's father was maybe not the person Book thought he was. Or, Moll has a very bad opinion of him, and he abandoned her. "Hey may have been a shit dad to you, but he was a great mentor to me." Uh, not really the best line, Book.  

Back in the present, they have a very tenuous truce. Which basically ends as soon as they do what they need to do, then it's back to fighting. I mean, the two of them aren't EVIL, they just are broken people who can't trust anyone. It kinda just sucks.
