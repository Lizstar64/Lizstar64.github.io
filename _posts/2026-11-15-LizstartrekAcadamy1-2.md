Every Sunday my Twitch subs and I watch Star Trek in my Discord. Here are my reviews and thoughts on each of the episodes as I see it. If you're curious about a nerd's views on Star Trek episodes, please read on, I go quite in depth with some of these episodes! I've watched some Star Trek before but not all of it!

Finally done with Discovery, thank god. It's time for the newest show of the whole lot, as of this writing, we're almost FINALLY caught up. Despite this being directly after Discovery and tying into it in many ways, I've heard good things about it. So let's be optimistic.

Though we did some research into pre-production and Kurtzman kept saying this was "the hip and cool Trek for the youths" and holy shit, dude, could you be any more Old Man?


**Series Acclimation Mil** - DIS
8.5/10

<img src="https://imgur.com/UgOSp63.png" alt="trek">

Holy shit we're getting episodes about specific characters. That's like, Old Trek shit. We're allowed to do that?! This episode is about Series Acclimation Mil, also known as Sam. She starts the episode by just... talking to you while walking through the commons. It's very interesting cause its directly from her point of view, and her point of view is pretty damn weird. She's walking through, shaking everyone's hands, and acting like the main character.

<img src="https://imgur.com/383GomO.png" alt="trek">

Basically, Sam was made by "the makers" who became sentient after being mistreated and used as slave labor by some species that died out ages ago. They want to open up and learn about others and be friends, but are also understandably hesitant, so Sam was went out to learn about Organics so she could explain them to them more easily. 

A lot of the episode is actually first person as she talks to people directly to try and learn about them. As we are WELL AWARE by now, Sam is... blunt, and unable to fully comprehend when to not talk, so she's just going up and asking Caleb "so you clearly want to have sex with her but do not, why is that?" I DO like Sam, but a lot of the humor with her is awkward humor, and that is 1000% not my jam.

<img src="https://imgur.com/3T3B7WQ.png" alt="trek">

Anyways, the plot of this episode is that the Makers want Sam to enroll in a specific class but when she approaches the teacher, she's told "dude it's midterms???" by this teacher, Illa. Illa is played by Mariner's actress btw, who is ALSO the writer, and the first Trek star to become a writer for Trek. But Sam wants to prove she can handle it and takes on a big project about Benjamin Sisko. REMEMBER HIM?? We get a big ol' lore update about Sisko and who he is. I know. It's my favorite Trek :) But it does a good enough job reminding you. Anyways she's like "oh, he was the Emissary. I'm an Emissary for my Makers! I must know what his deal is so I can become a good Emissary too". Maybe don't take Emissary lessons from Sisko.

She then offends every Bajoran in school by demanding they tell her if Sisko is alive or dead. Is... is this a metaphor for fans??

Our B plot is that Ake is trying to be nicer to Kelrec, who is currently... heating the pacific ocean so that an emissary from some planet he's trying to get on good terms with is happy. She's like "wtf you doing dog"

<img src="https://imgur.com/QAQIK0M.png" alt="trek">

I've always said I wanted a sequel to DS9. This isn't that, but it's continuing a lot of it and honestly it has some similarities (both take place in a static space and are a little more slice of life-y). Here's Jake! Nice to see him as grown up tbh. This is a hologram from the Sisko Museum, which notably also has a family tree on it that does NOT include Kassidy's unborn kid at the end of DS9. Is this.... dark confirmation about a miscarriage? New horrifying lore dropped.

Things aren't going well and the Makers are like "WE CREATED YOU WE CAN DECIDE WHAT TO DO WITH YOU AND IF YOU FAIL TO SHOW ANY OF THIS IS WORTH IT WE WILL LOCK YOU UP AND RETREAT FROM ORGANICS FOREVER". So uh... doing what their creators did to THEM, I see. Anyways, she has one week left. She spends this time going to the bar with her friends, cause Sisko went there once. She doesn't really know what she's doing but frankly neither do we.

Sam gets smashed at the club, and declares "Oh I get it. Being an emissary means you never get to do whatever you want, ever. Sisko probably just wanted to eat shrimp and grits and build sailboats" and I mean, yeah. But after she starts a bar fight and gets in trouble, she goes back to the teacher who gives her a nudge and hands her Jake Sisko's book. Sam then has a heart to heart in her mind with Jake, and then THIS drops

<img src="https://imgur.com/bU8fRl8.png" alt="trek">

Dax is STILL AROUND. Half Cardassian Half Trill, btw.

She ends up being like "I'm not QUITTING and will hit you up when I can to give you updates but hey I'm my own person, bai bitches" and then cuts off her Makers. Fuck yea, fuck them.

We end with a Sisko voice over. It's pretty nice. AND we end with the DS9 Theme!!! Hot damn.


**Come, Let's Away** - DIS
8/10

<img src="https://imgur.com/CTOHmPC.png" alt="trek">

This episode starts with an epileptic warning. Huh. Are you allowed to do anything you want if you warn people first? Time for red and blue flashing on every other frame!!

Caleb and Tarima fuck, and then have pillow talk about alien mating rituals, which is... fascinating. 

<img src="https://imgur.com/CBzUL6K.png" alt="trek">

That comes back fast though, as Betazoid's sex involves like, connecting with the other person's mind, and she sees a bunch of Caleb's past involving his mom, which he reacts VERY poorly to. He's offended by the idea that she got in his head without warning and permission but she's a fucking telepath, dude. Maybe she's not the right partner for you??? Anyways things are now VERY awkward.

The classes are assigned to a ship as a test exercise. Should be nice and simple with no major problems happening! They need to board a shut down ship and restore power. Caleb does it immediately cause he's a smarty pants for engineering. Aaand as soon as he does a bunch of ships show up and "claim it as salvage". They're weird cannibal pirates called the "Furies". They eat the lt cmdr who was teaching the kids on the ship, whoops!! It's really cronchy and wet sounding!

The War College Klingon who's been sassing them all season is like "A great man once said the needs of the many outweight the needs of the few, or the one" as a call to sacrifice himself. Hey, yeah, do you remember how is friends reacted to that? We got a whole fucking movie out of it.

<img src="https://imgur.com/zwT3lqF.png" alt="trek">

They need someone with experience beating the Furies. And you know who's beaten them before and knows their weaknesses? Paul Giamatti! I mean, Nus Braka. He's absolutely fucking nuts, I love him. He quotes Sound of Music and says something tastes like cat piss. He's a VERY interesting Klingon/Tellerite. He is 100% willing to work with them, IF they do him a favor. See, he was scamming people and had a toll road for who now can just use the dilithium that the Federation is giving them to go around him. He wants them to stop supplying Dilithium to these people. Kinda... fucked up and evil, but it IS a step below cannibalism, so we're still on the even ground here! Also he's got the hots for Ake and is a fucking creepy. Is... is thios going to suddenly veer into assault? God please do not.

Okay no, he's just... kinda mocking her about how her son died in a situation very simialr to this. What's your end goal, dude? Be an asshole? 

<img src="https://imgur.com/p85MDLS.png" alt="trek">

Tarima has a condition that makes it so her psychic abilities are WAY stronger than they should be. She can connect with Caleb and gives him information and orders, which she does. As they talk psychically, he apologizes for being a fuckass during sex. Not 100% the time, but your head is in the right place.

Also Braka finally ponies up. The Furies' weakness is they are part back, basically, and are very weak to sonic weapons. Those aren't very common, so they order some from a nearby weapons facility. I feel if that thing is nearby they could have... sent a lot of other things to kill them all, but this works too. They're gonna knock the Furies out, lower the shields, beam the kids out, and dip. ...Except yeah, Braka double crossed them, they were working together the whole time. Giamatti just wanted to get a bit extra on the side I guess. ALSO his raiders attacked the station they called reinforcements from now that it was weakened and ransacked it. Wow, if you can't trust the worst dude in space.

This does not go well. The Vulcan cadet is killed, Tarima rips her inhibitor so she can use her powers to help them, making psychic sonar weapons, basically. Just... Jean Grey-ing them to death, honestly. 

Admiral Sexypants puts out a massive bounty on his head. "No one sleeps until we hunt him down and end him". Yeah, I dunno wtf he was thinking, he's now at war. Hopefully this will be worth it, dude. He actually sends a message directly to Ake, saying how much he fucking hates her, and the Federation, because they "think they're better than him" and think he's a "lowlife" for just living his life the way he wants to. Also he says he's ready for them. I mean, your way of life is killing innocents to get yourself more things, so yeah. You are a lowlife :) It's a good speech, he's a fun villain.

8/10

<img src="https://imgur.com/UJp5BaG.png" alt="trek">

It's been a bit since the last episode. Tarima lived, but basically had a brain version of a heart attack and is recovering at home. Caleb can't send her a message, he's in his own mind too much. Sam is injured. Yes, Sam. Her holomatrix is damaged and is slowly being repaired. And Genesis is now on the fast track for captaincy. Wowee.

omfg. Okay, so, the war college kids? There are four main ones. There's Tarima. There's the vulcan and his girl, the vulcan died. And then there's Kyle. I haven't mentioned Kyle, but Kyle's deal is that the other war college kids are aggressive and kinda bullies and he's like "Hi guys :)" and is just a nice friendly guy. He and Kraag have been hanging out more and chilling together and they are fucking they are SO fucking they are very VERY fucking. It's honestly adorable. They're going on a trip to Ibiza! Darem is jealous >:(

<img src="https://imgur.com/Np8YEtS.png" alt="trek">

Everyone is packing up for the break and going home. omfg Ake packs her suitcase like I do. "YOU'RE TAKING FOUR PAIRS OF SHOES, AND YOU DON'T EVEN WEAR SHOES??" Anyways, Darem isn't planning to go home but then people come and kidnap him, using portal tech, which is NOT common in Trek. Kraag runs in after him, and suddenly finds himself as best man in his wedding. Which is a thing that happens with Kionians. Poor Jay-Den is out of his element. Darem's fiance is enby! Nice.

<img src="https://imgur.com/NMwyENv.png" alt="trek">

Genesis and Caleb both snuck back to campus because they don't really have anywhere else to be, and just play stupid games they make up. Genesis is like "so how's the girlfriend?" and Caleb is VERY bad at hiding he's bad at this. They accidentally let a Warp Slug out, and we get a big humorous scene with Reno having a conversation with her wife. Also they kill the poor slug ): Poor baby

Darem really needs his bro Jay-Den there, because he's struggling with this. The wedding wasn't going to be for a while, but they had to call it early cause he's marrying the princess and she needs to be queen NOW cause her parents are abdicating early to retire. He's basically giving up his entire life and personal goals out of duty and responsibility, and he will do it without whining, but he wants his bro there to help. It's a bit sad.

....okay, there is 100% something going on with Darem and Jay-Den. This is the gayest Trek, and all of it has actually been handled really well.

Neli made a good point in how this episode is the inverse of the traditional "take part in this Klingon ceremony even if it makes everything really awkward". I'd no joke love an episode where like, Worf had to go to a Bar Mitzvah and make an ass of himself.

Genesis uses Caleb to sneak onto the ship and steal some stuff so she can get access to her records to change things because without it, she'll be expelled, apparently. But we don't find out what that means cause Reno finds them and they are in the DEEPEST of shit. 

<img src="https://imgur.com/mIg6TOh.png" alt="trek">

Kraag storms off to find a way home but ends up deliverying the best man speech anyway. This speech rips the new queen apart. Because the person he was describing is someone she never met. And she realizes if he does this it's just to appease her and everyone else. So she asks him to abdicate and annuls the marriage. 

Genesis and Caleb just get menial labor, and Genesis is taken off of pre-command track. We also find out menial labor is literally just cleaning stuff. Genesis finally talks Caleb into not being an idiot and he messages his girlfriend.


**The Life of the Stars** - DIS
10/10

<img src="https://imgur.com/MCbBZK6.png" alt="trek">

Tarima is back at the acadamy. She's being transfered to Starfleet, instead of the War College. She's not handling the people hovering around her too well. She's... struggling, obviously. She's been pushed out of the war college because her condition will put her in danger if she gets more physical, so she only really has a future in non-war stuff.

The cadets are struggling to get together, and are not handling the trauma well. So they send in the big guns. 

<img src="https://imgur.com/n9fNGjF.png" alt="trek">

Tilly is their teacher for... THEATER CLASS. Yes, Tilly is FINALLY HERE! I thought this show would be MADE FOR HER, but instead she's here for a single episode as a guest star. wtf. Anyways no one is happy about this... except Sam, who is super excited.

This basically has four plots, all very important. Plot A) They are all doing theater and researching plays, they're gonna do Our Town. Tarima is assigned Emily. B) Caleb and Tarima are on the outs because Tarima is handling this so poorly. Caleb is awkward and doesn't know how to handle it. C) Sam is malfunctioning from being injured two episode ago and they're gonna go to her home planet to try and fix her. D) Jay-Den has a cold! :)

<img src="https://imgur.com/uFztbi6.png" alt="trek">

Tarima gets fucking wasted and finally reaches out out to Caleb. She's fucking druuuunk and finally comes back onto Caleb, but he's like "wait no, you're drunk, we should not" and she does noooot handle it well. "Oh you're afraid of being with the head melty girl? Afraid of fucking the monster?" No girl you're drunk. You can melt my head when you're sober though, hit me up. There's also stuff here about how obviously Caleb and Genesis are into each other, too. I was feeling hints of that last episode.

The way this episode handles the PTSD and fear of it all is actually pretty good. It's kind of a modern take on Only A Paper Moon. Maybe not as good, but still good. She has a breakdown in class about it and finally opens up to everyone.

<img src="https://imgur.com/OxoAYQ8.png" alt="trek">

The Kasque do a thing that's... kind of like the Great Link but for holograms? They "overwhelm" her and search through her data to see what's wrong with her. It's pretty creepy. They determine she is broken beyond repair. "What took us two hundred years to build you destroyed in 209 days." Damn.

We get a few really good scenes about this. Her friends are worried for her and act out the play Sam loved. Meanwhile, the Doctor explains why this is so tough on him. We get references to the episode where he made a hologram family. Holy SHIT I'm surprised we're calling back to that. We also learn the reason she's failing is actually NOT cause of the damage she took, but cause she wasn't built to properly process trauma and grief so it's cascading. They need to reprogram her with a childhood so she can properly handle the trauma, and the Doctor can be the parent! He is NOT happy about this. As he says too, he is a coward. But he finally agrees. Due to the way time works on Kasque, it'll only be a few days, but feel like 17 years for the Doctor and Sam.

We get scenes of them living together as daughter and father, with voice over from Occam from the play. It's... quite good!

Tilly gets a send off as Tarima basically thanks her for helping her move on. The episode ends with Ake basically as the Stage Manager from the play but for this show.

I feel like if I had read the play this would have been even better. It's kinda on the cusp of a 9.5 and a 10 but I'll be nice. This is prolly the last 10 Trek is ever getting anyway. Gotta be kind. I do prefer Paper Moon but y'know, I also had watched 7 seasons of Rom. I bet if this was season 7 this woulda kicked my ass even more.
