Every Sunday my Twitch subs and I watch Star Trek in my Discord. Here are my reviews and thoughts on each of the episodes as I see it. If you're curious about a nerd's views on Star Trek episodes, please read on, I go quite in depth with some of these episodes! I've watched some Star Trek before but not all of it!

Finally done with Discovery, thank god. It's time for the newest show of the whole lot, as of this writing, we're almost FINALLY caught up. Despite this being directly after Discovery and tying into it in many ways, I've heard good things about it. So let's be optimistic.

Though we did some research into pre-production and Kurtzman kept saying this was "the hip and cool Trek for the youths" and holy shit, dude, could you be any more Old Man?


**Kids These Days** - DIS
7.5/10

<img src="https://imgur.com/80O00Rk.png" alt="trek">

We're introduced to a small kid, Caleb Mir, who's in a waiting cell with his Mom, who's saying a whole lot of things that are saying "I am not going to be here for long". She also gives him a weird creepy toy.

Apparently the two of them were working with a pirate, named Paul Giamatti. Wait shit, I'm sorry, his name is Paul Giamatti. Fuck, I mean- Anyways, Paul Giamatti really REALLY wanted to play a Klingon villain, and you can tell, he's having the time of his life. He's sent to prison, and Caleb's mom is sent too for being an accomplice. Caleb then plays them for fools with the "I'm a little innocent kid~" shtick and breaks out of jail. This guy is gonna be our prococious scamp for the series who will have to learn the true meaning of friendship.

We then jump 15 years later. He's a prisoner again. Damn, dude, can't catch a break huh? He breaks free then is captured again. Maybe quit while you're ahead.

<img src="https://imgur.com/QfI1xJV.png" alt="trek">

We meet this lady, Nahla Ake, a half Human half Lanthanite. She was the captain who split up Caleb and his mom and lost him 15 years ago. She resigned over it cause she's like "what I was told to do what fucked up and I refuse to have anything to do with it." Admiral Sexypants from Discovery is like "hey we know where he is, maybe come out of retirement". So she does, she goes to pick Caleb up and gets him to sign a deal saying "do community service with me at Starfleet Acadamy, and I help you find your mom, who btw broke out of jail a year ago and hasn't even tried looking for you? That's fucked up"

PRetty good character motivations so far. Discovery also started with a criminal who has to be forced into servitude to help the Federation, but uh, Caleb is a kid? He's not an XO who makes dumb angry decisions for no reason. He's a KID who makes dumb decisons for no reason! I can get behind that way better.

<img src="https://imgur.com/JtIK63x.png" alt="trek">

We get lots of shotgunning jokes and scenes introducing the rest of the cast. Lura Thok, a Jem Hadar/Klingon hybrid who's a hardcore "YES SIR, STAND UP SOLDIER" type. "LET ME SEE YOUR WAR FACE!" Jay-Den, a Klingon cadet who's signed up for medical stuff because he wants to "live a valiant life". He also apologizes, which is a weird thing for a Klingon. I think I like his vibe already. Then of course, ya boi, the Doctor. I'm just happy to see him tbh. Also this Starfleet acadamy class takes place in a space ship that looks like the fucking Halo laser sword, and Ake sits in her chair in the most bisexual way I have ever seen.

Also, that's three hybrid characters so far I think?? Ake, Thok, and and Paul Giamatti are all hybrid peoples. That's rad tbh. More of that, please. This shit is a melting pot, let that pot MELT. No wonder this series pissed off fascists.

We get a scene of some rich kid bullying the Klingon for some fucking reason while everyone is like "WHAT ARE YOU DOING WHY ARE YOU DOING THAT" but only Caleb actually stands up for him and gets him his binoculars back. Jay-Den likes to go bird watching. I legit love this guy already??? He's basically a Klingon I would write.

<img src="https://imgur.com/RQkp9HT.png" alt="trek">

We also meet two other kids. Genesis, a kind of haughty but friendly girl, and SAM, a hologram. Or, as she prefers, "photonic". Ohhh, Genesis is actually kind of a FREAK. "I want to learn how to kill a man with a toothbrush". Caleb legit reacts by going "What the FUCK?" They're also ALREADY teasing a romance between these two. That was fast.

The ship flies into a place that immediately tries to blow it up. Why the fuck are we doing that??? This ship has kids on it. What is it, the Enterprise? A big spiky obviously evil ship is blowing them the fuck up. The cadets all gotta step up once Lura gets stabbed by some debris. Caleb knows a way to stop this, and starts to work with it. We also learn who the villain here is. It's Nus Braka, AKA Paul Giamatti! He's fun. Apparently Giamatti's favorite Trek is DS9 and you can tell cause he wants to be Gul Dukat. But like, a little less of a "convinces himself he's the good guy" and leaning a bit more into the "Gul Dukat was a weird creepy pervert". We also learn the reason he knows they're here is cause Caleb is trying to send messages to his mom. Everyone blames him but like, I dunno dog, he was just trying to send messages to his mom.

All of the cadets get time to shine here. Even the rich pretty boy, Darem, shines. He's "Khaonian", which allows him to um... Mystique himself and live in space. He's blue now, that's his attack. Oh they're also pushing stuff with him and Genesis. Time for a loooove trongle! Anyways Darem then almost freezes to death. I mean, that's not how heat works in space, he wouldn't radiate all that that fast, but that's okay. Genesis ends up saving him using a tractor beam.

There's actually some really good jokes here. A lot of them from Thok. "IF I AM GOING TO DIE TODAY, I NEED LESS CHEERFULNESS". And when she gives a friendly shoulder punch to Jay-Den, he just goes flyyyiiiing. Also I like Caleb's sass.

<img src="https://imgur.com/wpx8t83.png" alt="trek">

Caleb manages to do his thing and they blow up Giamatti's ship. He escapes in an escape pod, laughing maniacally as he does.

We then get a weird slowmo flyby of each of the characters as a weird slow version of "If You're Going To San Fransisco" plays. I've never been a huge fan of this kinda thing.

Captain Ake gives Caleb the chance to just... dip. He stays with them for 90 days and 100 hours of labor to pay off his jail time, and she helps him find his mom. OR he just leaves. He of course decides to stay, because she show has to happen.

I actually liked that. Honestly pretty up there for a first episode for a Trek series.

**Vox In Excelso** - DIS
6.5/10

<img src="https://imgur.com/bjXd2eg.png" alt="trek">

The Doctor is teaching debate. Jay-Den is NOT happy about this, he does not like public speaking. They do like, a tournament of debate. Caleb wins every single debate, hardcore. He's actually very good at debate. When it gets to Jay-Den, he just... has a panic attack. Poor Jay-Den.

I REALLY like Caleb and Jay-Den's friendship.

We also learn some Klingon lore. There were basically no Klingons in Discovery post time skip, so we learn a few things. The Burn caused Qo'noS to become uninhabitable and now Klingons are nearing extinction. That's... that's fucking nuts. What the fuck? They're showing this off through a flashback montage. What the fuck is it with Modern Star Trek and blowing up the capital planets of the other major forces? This is the first major worldbuilding mistep of all of this. And y'all KNOW how I love my Klingons, so...

Anyways we learn that Jay-Den's family might have died. And we get some flashbacks to him on a refugee planet. He was close with his brother, who supported him in what he wanted to do, but his dad didn't understand and wanted him to be a warrior like the rest of them. But his brother is cool as hell. "Your mind is hungry, brother. We must feed it!" He wants to get his bro some Federation tech.

<img src="https://imgur.com/zNO16kE.png" alt="trek">

We've got two major plots. One, the debate team is basically debating the rights of the Klingons to... exist? And that they should be helped to survive? Not sure what's going on there. Notably, Jay-Den is anti-Federation help. And Ake is going to try and talk to some Klingon leaders to offer them aid and help.

More flashbacks. Jay-Den's brother is killed trying to trade for Federation technology. Jay-Den is not allowed to try and save him because of his parents' bigotry.

<img src="https://imgur.com/9h0zfzk.png" alt="trek">

Ake meets up with an old... "friend". They were ABSOLUTELY fucking. They get smmmaaaashed. Okay yes, later on in a later scene, they were 100% fucking and it almost went somewhere but they were both too married to their jobs and cultures. A shame. They're cute together.

Of ALL the people to help psych up and therapy Jay-Den, it's Darem. Also it's very, VERY gay.

<img src="https://imgur.com/TUiyDF4.png" alt="trek">

It's now the day of the debate. Ake is hung over. It doesn't go well. Caleb VS Jay-Den ends with them yelling at each other Thok is like "Well I've never been an advisor for another Klingon and I don't wanna fuck it up but I'll do my best." She rights his views on his family and is like "They didn't abandon you, they let you go so you can be here in the only way they could". It's pretty nice.

Jay-Den goes back up to the debate stage so he can give a big debate about why the Federation shouldn't just give the Klingons what they need, and force them to stop being Klingons. It's a good scene, though I dunno why they have so many bright lights in my face. He ends up giving a new idea. "We should solve a Klingon problem with a Klingon solution."

What's that solution? Go to war with the Klingons. It's all one big excuse to let the Klingons win the planet as spoils of war. Also frankly it's foreplay for Ake and her klingon boytoy.

The episode is good. I do not like the stupid lore stuff with the Klingons but the writing and how they handled it in this episode were good. I just... man I wish we didn't have to deal with the Burn still, and all the nonsense that came with it.

We cut it to four episodes tonight cause holy SHIT why are these episodes an hour and 20 minutes?????
