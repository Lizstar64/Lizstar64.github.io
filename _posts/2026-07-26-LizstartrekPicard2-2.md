**Two of One** - PIC
4/10

<img src="https://imgur.com/hasdm5l.png" alt="trek">

We get another in media res thing where we see Picard dying. Okay, we keep doing that. In media res isn't just a cheat code, it needs to be good, too.

They're continuing the heist that was set up last episode. Everyone is in a fancy party trying to... somehow make sure that Picard's ancestor is still on the space mission. Dunno how they'll do that off the cuff but alright. Meanwhile, Jirati has a new headmate and it is going Very Poorly. 

<img src="https://imgur.com/gIwCiHw.png" alt="trek">

Soong comes up and tells Picard to leave. Nothing really comes from this at all. Then Jirati is taken over by the Borg Queen entirely and she takes this time to do a big musical number, so Picard can sneak time alone with Mama Picard. They have a heart to heart and then Soong just fucking runs him over. In a Tesla. Well, at least it was the correct person who did that. An asshole tech billionaire. They take him to the doctor who saved Rios that one time, and its solved immediately. Uh, okay. See, when I said In Media Res needs to be good, I meant it means to MEAN SOMETHING. Not be something solved immediately with no reprecusions. You could at least draw attention to the fact he's A FUCKING ROBOT. She's not bothered by that, at all.

<img src="https://imgur.com/NU7NZt5.png" alt="trek">

Soong is Sephiroth Posting around his house so his daughter googles him... or sorry, BINGS him for the first time and finds out that he is a mad scientist who cloned her, and in fact, she's like clone #6, all the others died.

Picard is stuck in his mind due to being hit by a car, sure. They can maybe wake him up, using the Watcher's mind probe. Meanwhile, Jirati is walking around as the Big Borg Bitch now, I'm sure that won't be a problem.



**Monsters** - PIC
3.5/10

<img src="https://imgur.com/PWQ2WCW.png" alt="trek">

We get a scene of Picard talking to some guy. He's some Federation guy, and they're doing a basic psych evaluation. He makes her tell him a story. It's making both of them uncomfortable. 

<img src="https://imgur.com/ceHalli.png" alt="trek">

He tells him a story that seems to be a retelling of a children's fairy tale, told to him as a kid by his mother, and re-enacted out by child picard and his mother. Young picard gets to watch his mother be dragged away into the darkness by a monster yay :)

Meanwhile, Picard is still suffering and dying. Seven and Raffi head out to find Jirati, who has gone MIA. But they're locked out of the ship by the Borg.

The Watcher uses some weird tech to mind meld with Picard so she can lead him out of his mind. So now the Watcher has to travel through the fantasy world in his mind and save child Picard's mother, because he refuses to leave without her. All while we ALSO continue to get the framing device of Picard getting increasingly annoyed at his psychiatrist.

Meanwhile Picard and the Watcher appear to be having strokes so Rios has to rope the doctor into the whole space thing. "Are you from... outer space?" "Mmm? No, no, I'm from Chile. I just.... work in space." A good line.

<img src="https://imgur.com/RmvB6IK.png" alt="trek">

Turns out this is all daddy issues. The psychiatrist is actually his dad, who looks like an older Bashir tbh. We get some backstory that explains Picard's parents. She got locked in a dark room for a while and went fucking nuts. He blamed his dad because he was a little kid. She clearly had some kind of disorder that y'know, could be easily treated cause it's 2300. The way it's all framed makes it seem like it's the 1800s, but my man's from the 2300s.

<img src="https://imgur.com/6EQberq.png" alt="trek">

Now awake, Picard decides he needs to talk to Q. Like, actually talk, maybe he can figure something out. So he asks Guinan. Turns out she DOES have a way to summon Q. She has THE TRUCE BOTTLE, a magical bottle of wine that was used during the truce between the El-Aurians and the Q. I guess she just HAS that. Well, okay, she SAYS she can do it but when she tries it just doesn't work.

Meanwhile, Seven discovers what exactly Jurati is doing. Well, the Queen in Jurati's body. She's forcing endorphins so that the assimilation process speeds up. I guess that happens.

The episode ends with an FBI agent arresting Picard after showing that they saw him teleport. Man, they just CAN'T not fuck up the timeline, can they??? God knows whats going on in the future right now.
